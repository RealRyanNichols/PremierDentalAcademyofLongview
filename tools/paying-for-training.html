How people actually pay for it

Seven routes, and for each one the thing nobody tells you: who makes the decision. Some are ours. Most are not — and knowing which is which saves you weeks of ringing the wrong number.

Read this bit first

We can tell you what each route is and who runs it. We cannot tell you whether you will be approved for any of them, because for most of these the decision is not ours to make. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. Where the decision is ours, we have said so plainly.

1. Pay in full

In-person training is $3,000 paid up front. That is the whole cost of tuition — there is no interest and nothing added later.

Who decides: nobody. You pay, you are enrolled.

2. Our payment plan

$3,500 total on a plan: $500 down holds your seat, then the $3,000 balance in weekly or monthly instalments — up to about 13 payments across 12 months. The $500 difference from paying in full is the cost of spreading it out. Your certificate is issued once tuition is paid in full.

Who decides: we do. This is our own plan, there is no credit check, and no third-party lender is involved. Ring (903) 913-6444 and ask for Amanda.

3. Workforce Solutions (WIOA)

The Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act funds job training for people who qualify — often those who have been laid off, are on a low income, or are changing careers. Where it applies it can cover a substantial part of training costs. Start at your local Workforce Solutions East Texas office, or dial 211.

Who decides: Workforce Solutions, not us. They assess your eligibility against their own criteria and their available funding. We can send them whatever paperwork they need about the programme, but we have no say in the outcome and no way to speed it up. Apply early — their process takes time.

4. Texas Vocational Rehabilitation

The Texas Workforce Commission runs Vocational Rehabilitation Services, which helps people with a disability that affects their ability to work get into employment — training costs included, for those who qualify.

Who decides: the Texas Workforce Commission, through a VR counsellor assigned to you. You apply to them directly. We are not part of that assessment.

5. Your employer

Dental offices sometimes pay to train someone they already trust — a front-desk person moving to the back, or a hire they want registered. It happens more often than people expect, and it is almost always the employee who has to raise it.

Who decides: your employer. We are happy to talk to them, send a cost breakdown, or invoice the practice directly — but the ask has to come from you first.

6. Split-pay providers

Klarna, Afterpay and Affirm let you spread a payment over time. They are independent companies with their own terms, and depending on the plan they may charge interest or fees. Read what you are agreeing to before you sign.

Who decides: the provider, usually on a credit check at checkout. If they decline you, that is their decision and it does not affect your standing with us at all — our own payment plan is still open to you.

7. Sponsor a student

Local people and businesses sometimes put money towards a student's tuition through our sponsor-a-student page. Availability depends entirely on whether anyone has sponsored recently, so there is no queue to join and no promise attached.

Who decides: we do, when funds exist. Ask — but plan as though the answer is no, and treat it as a bonus if it is not.

The cost, plainly

  • In-person, paid in full: $3,000. About 12 weeks of daytime classes at our Longview campus.
  • In-person, on a plan: $3,500. $500 down plus a $3,000 balance in weekly or monthly instalments.
  • Online: $397 (regular price $997). This programme is distance education — studied entirely online at your own pace, without hands-on chair time.

To complete the programme you need to finish the coursework and the required hands-on competencies for the in-person programme, and tuition must be paid in full before your certificate is issued.

Transfer and refund policy in full: Terms. Online tuition is non-refundable but transfers as credit toward in-person tuition within 90 days; in-person tuition is pro-rated per the Terms. Premier Dental Academy of Longview holds Texas Workforce Commission Career School Licence #S5316.

Discounts are not advertised and there are no promo codes. If cost is the thing standing in your way, say so on the phone — (903) 913-6444 — and we will talk about it honestly.
