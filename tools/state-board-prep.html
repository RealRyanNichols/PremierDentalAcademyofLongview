What a session looks like

  • You pick the focus. Infection control, radiology, charting, instruments, the jurisprudence portion — whatever is making you nervous, that's what we work on.
  • Real practice questions, out loud. Original questions in the style of the exam, worked through together until the reasoning clicks — not just memorized answers.
  • A plan you leave with. What to study in your remaining days, in what order, and how to check yourself.
  • In person or remote. Come to the Longview campus and use the real equipment, or meet by phone/video from anywhere in Texas.

Open to everyone preparing for the Texas RDA exam — our current students, our graduates, and students from other programs. This is an educational review session with original materials; it is not the official state exam, and completing it is separate from Texas RDA registration itself.
