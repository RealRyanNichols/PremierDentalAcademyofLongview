What this costs at Premier Dental Academy

In-person training is $3,000 paid in full, or $3,500 on a payment plan — $500 down holds your seat, then the $3,000 balance in weekly or monthly instalments. The programme runs about 12 weeks at our Longview campus. Your certificate is issued once tuition is paid in full.

The online programme is $397 (regular price $997) and is distance education — it is studied entirely online at your own pace and does not include hands-on chair time.

Transfer and refund terms are set out in full on our Terms page. Questions about cost are welcome before you enrol, not after — call or text (903) 913-6444.
