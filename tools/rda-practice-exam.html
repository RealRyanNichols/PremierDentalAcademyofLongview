About the passing standard

DANB reports exam results as a scaled score, not a percent correct. A scaled score of at least 400 is required to pass, on a scale of 100 to 900. Texas does not publish a percentage passing score for the state RDA exam — the passing standard is set by your TSBDE-approved course provider.

That is why this exam gives you a raw count and a per-topic breakdown and stops there. Converting practice results into a predicted pass would mean inventing a number, and you would be entitled to be annoyed when it turned out to be wrong.
