How much time the programme actually asks for

Our in-person programme runs about 12 weeks of daytime classes at the Longview campus. We are not going to print class hours here that might be out of date by the time you read them — call or text (903) 913-6444 and we will tell you the current days and times for the cohort you are looking at, or see the class calendar.

The online programme is self-paced distance education — you fit the study around your week rather than the other way round. It is studied entirely online and does not include hands-on chair time.

In-person tuition is $3,000 paid in full or $3,500 on a plan; online is $397 (regular $997). Refund and transfer terms are on the Terms page.
