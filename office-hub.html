Common questions

Is the plan generator really free?

Yes. No account, no card. You answer four questions, you get the full plan on screen, and you can print it or have it emailed to you. It's useful on its own even if you never open a workspace.

Do you store patient information?

No — and we designed it so we can't. The hub has no fields or uploads for patient data, I-9/W-4 forms, SSNs, health records, background checks, or payroll. It's a training record, nothing else.

Who can see our workspace?

Your team, by role. Premier's team can only look in if you explicitly grant support access — the grant expires automatically, you can revoke it any time, and every support view is written to an audit log your office can read.

Does a sign-off certify my employee?

No. Sign-offs are your office's internal record that your trainer watched a skill done to your standard. They are not a license or certification, and they don't replace state-required training.
