Official Texas registration resources

The Texas State Board of Dental Examiners (TSBDE) is the registration authority. Its current application instructions and rules control the state process. Premier Dental Academy provides education and practice tools; training completion and state registration are separate steps.

TSBDE · Application

RDA registration and X-rays

The Board’s checklist covers high-school or GED eligibility, fingerprints, hands-on BLS, approved course/exam or qualifying DANB documentation, and other required records. Start here to understand the application that fits your situation.

Read the application requirements ↗
TSBDE · Provider list

Approved courses and exams

Check the Board’s current list of approved X-ray certification courses and examinations. Ask your training provider which exam and completion documents are included, then compare those details with the Board’s application instructions.

TSBDE · Assessment

Texas jurisprudence assessment

The Board’s application guide explains when a jurisprudence assessment is required, including the DANB pathway and older course/exam results. Use the official assessment page for the available assessment and its current instructions.

Open the official assessment page ↗
TSBDE · Renewal

Continuing education and BLS

The Board lists six CE hours each year, including at least three clinical hours. Its two-year renewal chart totals twelve hours, including six clinical hours, and lists hands-on BLS. Check the current human-trafficking training requirements too.

Review CE and renewal guidance ↗
TSBDE · Additional training

Nitrous oxide monitoring

Monitoring has a separate application and specific training requirements. The Board describes at least eight hours of approved instruction and testing. An active RDA registration is required before applying; check the Board’s course and application details.

Read the monitoring requirements ↗
TSBDE · Current rules

Dental rules and infection control

Use the Board’s laws-and-rules hub to reach current dental-assistant rules and the Dental Practice Act. Review the official requirements alongside your infection-control and radiology study materials, and confirm task-specific requirements with the Board.

Read the current laws and rules ↗
