The quick answer: all six dates

Six in-person cohorts start at the Longview campus this fall — two class-day patterns, three windows:

August: Monday/Wednesday/Friday starting or Tuesday/Thursday starting September 29.
September: Monday/Wednesday/Friday starting September 14, or Tuesday/Thursday starting September 29.
November: Monday/Wednesday/Friday starting November 9, or Tuesday/Thursday starting November 17.

All classes are daytime classes.

Exact hours depend on your cohort — call or text (903) 913-6444 and we'll walk you through the schedule for the date you're eyeing. The calendar page always shows what's coming next.

MWF or Tuesday/Thursday — which rhythm fits you?

Both patterns cover the same ~12-week program, the same skills, the same certificate. The difference is purely the shape of your week. A Monday/Wednesday/Friday cohort spreads training across three days, which many students find easier to absorb — shorter gaps between classes, steady momentum. A Tuesday/Thursday cohort keeps five days a week free for work, kids, or everything else, which is why it's a favorite for students holding down a job while they train.

Neither is "better." Pick the one your real weekly life can sustain for twelve weeks — that's the honest test. If you're working while training, here's how students make that work.

August, September, or November?

August is the soonest path: start in a couple of weeks, finish in mid-November, and be job-hunting before the year ends. September gives you a few weeks to arrange work schedules, childcare, or funding paperwork first — and still wraps by roughly mid-December. November suits planners: lock in your seat now, settle your fall, train through the winter, and walk into the new year with a new career underway.

One thing to know either way: classes are capped at eight students. That's a promise about attention, not a pressure tactic — but it does mean a specific date can genuinely fill. If your heart is set on one cohort, don't sit on it for weeks. More on choosing timing: September or November?

What the twelve weeks actually look like

Training happens in a space built to feel like a working dental office, because that's what you're being prepared for. You'll practice with real instruments and equipment, learn tooth and perio charting, tray setups and sterilization, patient scheduling, clinical notes, and the insurance paperwork — ADA claims and EOBs — that makes you genuinely useful from week one on the job. You can try pieces of it yourself right now in the Skills Lab and the free Practice Pro trainer.

Cost, in plain numbers

Tuition is the same for every cohort: $3,000 paid in full, or $3,500 on a payment plan — $500 down to hold your seat, then the $3,000 balance in simple weekly or monthly payments. No surprise fees between those two numbers. The free tuition planner lets you build your exact payment schedule before you commit.

Funding help is real here.

We accept Texas Workforce Solutions, WIOA, and GI Bill funding. If money is the thing holding you back, ask before you rule anything out — start with how WIOA funding works in East Texas.

Three quick questions everyone asks

"Is it too late for the August dates?" Not as of this writing — but with eight seats per class, August is always the first to tighten up. The honest move is to apply or call now and ask exactly what's open, rather than guessing from a webpage. The calendar stays current.

"Do I need any experience first?" No. The program is built for beginners — that's the whole point of the twelve weeks. If you're nervous about starting from zero, read this honest answer about starting with no experience.

"Does finishing make me an RDA?" Finishing the program and becoming a Registered Dental Assistant in Texas are two separate steps — the RDA registration goes through the state. We prepare you for it, and you can warm up anytime with the free practice exam tool. For official requirements, always check tsbde.texas.gov.

If fall genuinely doesn't work

Life doesn't always cooperate with a cohort calendar. The online program is self-paced and starts any day you do — it's $997. It's the same curriculum knowledge delivered fully online, so it isn't physical hands-on training, but every dollar of it transfers as credit toward in-person tuition within 90 days if you decide to come train in the building.
