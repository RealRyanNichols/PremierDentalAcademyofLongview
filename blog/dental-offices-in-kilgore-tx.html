Kilgore's dental scene, in one paragraph

Kilgore keeps its dentistry close to home: two family practices anchor the older part of town on Broadway and Kay Street, a kids-focused clinic sits right on Highway 259, and the Stone Road corridor has quietly become the town's dental row with three practices within a mile of each other. Between them, Kilgore residents can get general checkups, cosmetic work, implants, dentures, and children's dentistry without ever leaving Gregg County — and when offices this close together compete for good staff, trained dental assistants benefit. That's where our Longview campus up Highway 259 comes in.

Looking for a dentist in Kilgore? A quick word

This list exists because people search for it — and because we drive through Kilgore all the time. A few honest pointers: most practices here are small teams, so call ahead rather than walking in; if you have kids, both the kids-focused clinic on 259 and the family practices see young patients; and if a practice's schedule is full, the next one is rarely more than five minutes away. Details like hours and insurance networks change often, so treat the phone numbers above as your first step.
