What should I do after high school? A 2-minute honest quiz

Nobody can tell you what to do with your life from five multiple-choice questions — so this quiz won't pretend to. What it will do is take how you actually like to work and hand you a direction worth exploring first, plus the honest next steps for it. Two minutes, no login, and your answers stay on this page unless you choose to send them.

Before you start: what this is and isn't

This is a reflection tool we built, not a scientific assessment — think of it as the structured version of a good conversation with someone who asks better questions than "so what's your plan?" It sorts you toward one of four directions based on how you like to work. Take the result as a starting point to research, not a prophecy. If you're under 18, do the exploring it suggests together with a parent — the follow-up steps involve real decisions and real money, and those go better with your people in the room.
