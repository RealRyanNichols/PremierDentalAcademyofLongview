Dental assistant school near Center, TX

If you are in Shelby County and you have started looking at dental assisting, the first useful thing we can tell you is a geography fact, not a sales pitch. Our campus is on Gilmer Road in Longview, and that is a real drive from Center. So let's start there, honestly, and work out which of the two ways in actually fits your life.

First, the part most schools would bury

Center sits down in the southeast corner of East Texas, and Longview does not. Depending on which roads you take and where in Shelby County you start, you are looking at roughly an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half each way — and that is an estimate, not a promise. Put your own address into a map app before you decide anything. Your number is the only one that matters, and it is the number your mornings will actually be built around.

We could have opened this page with how much you would love it here. We would rather open with the drive, because the wrong answer at this stage almost never looks like a bad school. It looks like a capable person who quits in week five because the road ate everything that was left of her day.

Two honest options from Center: the online program, which is self-paced and can start any day from your own kitchen table — or the in-person class in Longview, if the drive genuinely fits your week. Most people at this distance should look hard at the first one before they talk themselves into the second.

Why online goes first on this list instead of last

A lot of schools treat their online option as the consolation prize — the thing they mention once the campus pitch has not landed. At your distance that is backwards. The online program covers the same curriculum, it is self-paced, and it starts whenever you are ready rather than whenever the next class fills. Nobody drives anywhere.

That matters more than it sounds like it does. The students who finish are almost never the ones with the most free time. They are the ones whose plan survived a flat tire, a sick kid, and a schedule change at work in the same month. A plan with ninety minutes of highway built into every class day has a lot less room to absorb a bad week than one you can pick up at nine at night.

You can read more about how the two formats differ in online vs. in-person dental assistant training, and see the program itself on the online course page.

And the part of that we will not oversell

Online is coursework and software practice. It is not physical hands-on training, and we are not going to pretend otherwise — you are not holding an instrument, and nobody is standing over your shoulder telling you your finger rest is wrong. Anyone who tells you their online course is the same as being in a room with equipment is selling you something.

Ask yourself plainly: do you learn better reading and repeating on your own schedule, or do you learn by doing a thing badly in front of someone who can correct you on the spot? Neither answer is the wrong one. But they point to different programs, and knowing which one you are is worth more than any brochure.

If you want to be in the room: the drive, plainly

Our in-person classes are daytime, and the cohorts run on one of two formats. The approved class hours are Mon/Wed/Fri, 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM or Tue/Thu, 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM, depending on which class you join. There are no evening, night or Saturday classes — not sometimes, not by request.

From Shelby County, that difference is not a detail, it is the whole decision. A Mon/Wed/Fri class is a shorter morning but three round trips a week. A Tue/Thu class is a longer day but only two. At a long distance, fewer trips usually wins — the highway, not the classroom, is what wears people down. Sit with your own calendar and count the weeks, not the days.

The money question, with no number in this post

You will notice we have not typed a price anywhere on this page. That is deliberate. Tuition, payment plans and what is included live in one place on this site so they can never drift out of date in an old blog post — see the enrollment page for current pricing, and the tuition planner if you want to see what a payment plan would look like against your own budget.

Funding is worth asking about before you assume you cannot afford this. Texas Workforce Solutions, WIOA and veterans benefits are all things we work with, but eligibility is decided by those agencies, not by us — so the honest advice is to call your local Workforce Solutions office and ask, rather than take any school's word for what you qualify for. The funding finder will point you at the right doors.

Check us, and everyone else, before you pay

This is a career you are buying, so do the verification. Premier Dental Academy of Longview holds TWC Career School License #S5316, which you can confirm yourself through the Texas Workforce Commission rather than taking it from a website. And be clear-eyed about what a school can and cannot hand you: finishing here earns a certificate of completion from us. Registration as a dental assistant in Texas is a separate process run by the state, and the current requirements live at tsbde.texas.gov — always check them at the source, because they change and a confident blog post quoting last year's version is worse than none at all.
