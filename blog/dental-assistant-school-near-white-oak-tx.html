What the training actually looks like

The campus is built to feel like a working dental office, because that's what you're training for. You practice on real equipment and instruments, run patient scenarios in Practice Pro (the same style of practice-management software real offices run), and build chairside skills step by step in the Skills Lab. By the time you sit a working interview, the software, the trays, and the routines already feel familiar.
