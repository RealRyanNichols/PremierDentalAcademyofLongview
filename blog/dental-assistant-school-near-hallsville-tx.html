The short version

There's no dental assisting school inside Hallsville, and there doesn't need to be — the nearest hands-on program is a straight shot up US-80. Our campus at 2800 Gilmer Rd, Suite 106 in Longview trains Registered Dental Assistant (RDA) candidates in about twelve weeks, with classes capped at eight students so nobody gets lost in the back row. Cohorts meet in the daytime, two or three days a week depending on which start date you pick — call or text (903) 913-6444 for exact class hours before you plan around work or kids.

We keep a dedicated Hallsville page with commute notes, program details, and start dates in one place. This article is the longer, more honest version of that conversation.

The drive, honestly

You already know the road: US-80 west toward Longview, the same drive folks make for work, groceries, and Friday games. The campus sits on Gilmer Rd (Highway 300) on the northwest side of Longview, so from Hallsville you'll cross town or loop around — for most people it lands somewhere around twenty-five minutes door to door, depending on where you live and the time of day. Not nothing, but very doable two or three days a week for twelve weeks. Students commute to us from Marshall every cohort, and Hallsville is closer than Marshall.

Daytime classes only

What the training actually looks like

The campus is set up to feel like a working dental office, because that's the job you're training for. You practice on real equipment and instruments, build tray setups and chairside routines in the Skills Lab, and run patient scenarios in Practice Pro — the same style of practice-management software real offices run, from the tooth chart to scheduling to clinical notes. By the time you interview, the software and the routines already feel familiar instead of foreign.

Cost, in plain numbers

Two ways to pay for in-person training

$3,000 paid in full, or $3,500 on a plan — $500 down holds your seat, then the $3,000 balance in simple weekly or monthly payments. No surprise fees. Full breakdown: how the payment plan works.

You may not have to pay all of it yourself

We accept Texas Workforce Solutions, WIOA, and GI Bill funding. If money is the sticking point, start with WIOA funding in East Texas, then call and we'll help you figure out what you qualify for.

The Hallsville advantage: two job markets, not one

Here's the part most people miss. Hallsville sits on US-80 between Longview and Marshall, which means when you finish training you can job-hunt in both directions without moving or making a painful commute. Longview has the bigger cluster of offices; Marshall has its own steady demand — and smaller-town offices often have the hardest time finding trained assistants, which works in your favor as a new RDA. Hallsville even has dental offices of its own; you can browse who's practicing nearby in our East Texas dental office directory.
