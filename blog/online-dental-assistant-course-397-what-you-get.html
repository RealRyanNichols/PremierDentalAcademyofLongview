What online is not

We won't pretend otherwise

The online course is not physical hands-on training. You will not be seating patients, passing instruments, or taking X-rays from your couch — nobody can teach your hands over the internet, and any program that claims to is overselling. Online gives you the knowledge layer. The hand skills come from practice in a real operatory, whether that's our Longview campus or on the job.

That's exactly why the 90-day credit exists. Some students start online, realize they want the equipment and the in-room coaching, and upgrade. Others finish online while working, then build hand skills with their first employer. Both paths are legitimate — we just want you choosing with clear eyes.
