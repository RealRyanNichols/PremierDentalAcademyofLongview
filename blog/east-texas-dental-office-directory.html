What this directory is

A free, public reference of dental practices within roughly 50 miles of Longview — general dentistry, pediatric, orthodontics, oral surgery, endodontics, and community clinics — compiled from public business listings and practice websites in July 2026. Each city page lists the office name, address, phone, website when available, and years in business where public records show it. No rankings, no pay-to-play, no ratings — just the map.

Why a dental assisting school keeps this list.

Every office on these pages is a potential employer for our graduates and a potential training partner for us. Our students use this directory to map their job search; offices use it to be found. That alignment is why we maintain it and keep it free.
