What this list tells you about working in dental

Twenty-six practices in one mid-sized Texas city — general dentists, pediatric specialists, orthodontists, an oral surgery center, and an endodontics office. Several have served Longview for 30, 40, even 50 years. Every single one runs on dental assistants: charting, sterilization, chairside support, scheduling, claims.
