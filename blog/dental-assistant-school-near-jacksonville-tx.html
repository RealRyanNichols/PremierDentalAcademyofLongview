Dental assistant school near Jacksonville, TX

The short version

Premier Dental Academy of Longview is at 2800 Gilmer Rd, Suite 106, in Longview. From Jacksonville that is roughly an hour, depending on whether you run US-79 through Henderson and up US-259, or head northeast through Tyler. Neither route is dramatic; both are ordinary East Texas highway driving.

The in-person program runs about twelve weeks, every class is capped at eight students, and new cohorts start every few weeks instead of once a semester. If the drive does not work for your life, the online program covers the same curriculum from home and starts any day. We would genuinely rather you pick the one you will finish.

Your three real options, honestly

When you live in a town without a program, the choice usually gets framed as “is it worth the drive.” That is the wrong question, because it only compares one option against zero. Here are all three, with who each one is genuinely wrong for.

Commute

Best if you want hands on real equipment and a person correcting you in the moment. Wrong for you if your week has no dependable four-hour block or the fuel math genuinely does not close.

Online

Best if your schedule is unpredictable or the drive is out of reach. Wrong for you if you already know you will not open the course without someone expecting you somewhere.

Wait

Sometimes the right call — a move, a baby, a job about to change. Wrong for you if “later” has already been the answer for two years running.

Nobody needs to be talked into a career change. If any of the three is obviously yours, that is useful information and you can stop reading here.

What the drive is honestly like

We are not going to pretend a commute is free. An hour each way is two hours of your day on every class day, plus fuel, and over twelve weeks that is real time and real money. You should count it before you enroll rather than discover it in week four.

What makes it manageable is what you are driving toward. A class of eight means you are not standing in line for equipment — you are using it. The small-class piece goes into what that actually changes minute to minute. Every drive buys chair time, not lecture time.

Practical advice from people who have done it: drive the route once at the hour you would really be driving it, before you sign anything. An hour on a map and an hour on a Tuesday morning with coffee and a kid in the back seat are not always the same hour.

Do the fuel math with your own numbers

We are not going to invent a gas price for you or guess what your truck gets. Put in your own figures and look at the twelve-week total honestly. If it is uncomfortable but survivable, that is normal. If it is genuinely not possible, the online route exists for exactly this reason.

Two schedules, two very different commutes

Classes are daytime. Which schedule you get depends on the cohort you join:

  • Mon/Wed/Fri cohorts — 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM. Three mornings a week, finished by lunch.
  • Tue/Thu cohorts — 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Two longer days, and the rest of the week is yours.

From an hour out, that difference is bigger than it looks on paper. Three mornings is six one-way trips a week; two days is four. If you are working, the two-day pattern is usually easier to negotiate with a manager. If you have school-age kids, the morning pattern often lands better because you are home in the afternoon. Neither is the harder program — the hours add up the same way. There is a fuller comparison in which class schedule fits your life.

Those two lines are the only class hours we publish. If you find evening or Saturday times attached to our name on a directory somewhere, that listing is out of date. Call or text (903) 913-6444 and we will tell you straight.

What you are actually driving for

The reason people make this drive is that the first day in a real dental office is not hard because of the dentistry. It is hard because of the software, the charting, the insurance, and the pace — none of which a textbook teaches.

So we built our own trainers rather than licensing a generic course platform. Practice Pro is the front-desk and business side: scheduling, ledger, claims, recall. ChairSide is the clinical workflow: patient banner, notes, perio summary. You can try both from Jacksonville tonight, for free, before you decide anything — that alone will tell you more about whether this work suits you than any brochure will. The Skills Lab shows the rest.

Paying for it

Current tuition, the pay-in-full option, and the payment-plan terms all live on the enrollment page — that page reads from the same system that runs checkout, so it is always the accurate one. If you want to see what a plan looks like spread across weeks before you talk to anybody, the tuition planner builds the exact schedule.

Funding worth asking about: Texas Workforce Solutions and WIOA, and GI Bill benefits for veterans and eligible spouses. We accept all three. Eligibility is decided by those agencies, not by us, so the honest first step is a phone call to your local Workforce Solutions office — and it is worth making before you rule the program out on cost.

We are licensed by the Texas Workforce Commission as a career school, license #S5316, which you can verify yourself in the TWC directory. Completing our program earns a Premier Dental Academy certificate of completion; state registration as a dental assistant is a separate process run by the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners, and their site is the authority on current requirements.

Nearby, if you are comparing

Cherokee County students usually end up weighing Longview against Tyler. We are not going to run down anyone else's program — plenty of people have trained well at other schools. Ask any program the same questions and go with whoever answers them specifically: how many students per class, how many times you will personally touch the equipment, what software you practice on, and what the class hours are in writing.

If you are mapping your options, these neighbors cover similar ground: Henderson, Nacogdoches, and Overton.
