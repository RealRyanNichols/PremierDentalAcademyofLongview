Pittsburg sits in that in-between spot on US-271 — Mount Pleasant up the road, Gilmer down it, and no dental assistant program in any of them. The nearest real classroom is ours in Longview. Here's what that actually means for a Camp County student.

The geography, honestly

We are in Longview at 2800 Gilmer Rd, Suite 106 — Longview only, no Pittsburg location, and we won't pretend otherwise. From Pittsburg you head down US-271 through Gilmer and you're at our door; it's the same corridor Camp County commuters already run for work and shopping. Classes meet two or three days a week (Mon/Wed/Fri or Tue/Thu cohorts), daytime — call or text (903) 913-6444 for exact hours.

Is the drive worth it? The honest math

For twelve weeks, yes or no depends on your life, not our opinion. What the drive buys: an eight-seat class where the instructor knows by Tuesday if you're struggling, real instruments and chairs, and software trainers that make offices comfortable hiring you. What it costs: gas and time, two or three days a week, for twelve weeks — then it's done. Put your real numbers in the take-home pay tool and see what the career pays back against the commute; for wage questions, our salary page gives East Texas estimates with honest caveats.

Camp County carpool math

Two students from the Pittsburg area in the same cohort = half the gas and a study partner. When you apply, ask whether anyone from your side of US-271 is starting the same date — Amanda will tell you.

The no-commute option

If the drive is a dealbreaker, the self-paced online program delivers the same 12-module curriculum at home, starts any day, and carries a 90-day full-credit transfer to in-person if you change your mind. Current pricing for both is on /enroll — note the online program is at its regular price now; there is no sale to wait for.

Paying for it from Camp County

Check funding before you assume out-of-pocket: the free funding finder takes two minutes, and the WIOA guide explains the East Texas process. Workforce Solutions, WIOA, and GI Bill are all accepted.

Do this before deciding anything

Make the drive once, on a tour. You'll know within thirty minutes whether the room is worth twelve weeks of US-271 — and if it isn't, the online door stays open.
