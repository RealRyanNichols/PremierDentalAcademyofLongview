Dental assistant school near Gilmer, TX: your real options

The short answer

Gilmer doesn't have a dental assisting school inside the city limits — most towns its size don't. So people in Gilmer, Big Sandy, Diana, Union Grove, and the rest of Upshur County really have two options: commute to a hands-on program in Longview, or train online from home. Both are legitimate paths to working chairside. The right one depends on how you learn and what your weeks look like.

Why this drive is easier than most

Our campus is at 2800 Gilmer Rd, Suite 106 — on Longview's northwest side, the side facing Gilmer. Coming down from Upshur County, you reach us before you ever hit crosstown Longview traffic. Most folks call it about half an hour door to door.

Option 1: hands-on training, 30-ish minutes south

Our in-person program in Longview runs about 12 weeks and is built to feel like a working dental office rather than a lecture hall. You train on real equipment and instruments, practice charting on an anatomical tooth chart, run schedules on a drag-and-drop appointment board, write SmartDoc clinical notes, and work insurance claims the way a real front office does — the same systems in our Practice Pro trainer and Skills Lab.

Classes are capped at eight students, which matters more than any brochure line: when there are eight of you, nobody hides in the back row, and nobody graduates without actually holding the instruments. Classes run in the daytime — call or text (903) 913-6444 for exact hours. Cohorts meet either Monday/Wednesday/Friday or Tuesday/Thursday, which is exactly what the commute calculator below is for.

Option 2: train online from Gilmer for $997

If the drive doesn't work for your season of life — little kids, a job you can't leave yet, one shared vehicle — the online course covers the same curriculum, fully self-paced, and starts any day you do. It's $997 right now.

Two honest notes. First, online is not hands-on training — you won't be holding instruments until you're in an office. Second, the $997 isn't a dead end: if you later decide you want the in-person experience, every dollar transfers as credit toward in-person tuition within 90 days. Plenty of people start online to prove to themselves they love the material, then join a cohort.

What each path costs

Straight pricing, no asterisks

In-person: $3,000 paid in full, or $3,500 on a payment plan — $500 down holds your seat, then the $3,000 balance in simple weekly or monthly payments. Online: $997. Map your exact payment schedule with the tuition planner.

If money is the obstacle, don't cross yourself off the list before asking questions. Workforce Solutions East Texas serves Upshur County, and we accept WIOA funding and VA education benefits when a student qualifies — approval is always the agency's call, not ours, but the asking is free. Is it worth the drive?

Twelve weeks of commuting is a season, not a lifestyle — and it buys a skill that works anywhere in East Texas. Your classmates already make this same trade from White Oak and Gladewater, and Gilmer's drive isn't much different. When it's done, you're qualified for dental offices in Longview, Gilmer itself, and every town between — close to home, in a field that doesn't lay people off when the economy sneezes.
