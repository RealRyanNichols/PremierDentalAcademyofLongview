1. What a dental assistant actually does

A dental assistant runs the operatory: seating and calming patients, setting instrument trays, passing instruments in four-handed dentistry, keeping the field dry, charting teeth, sterilizing instruments, taking X-rays (with the right registration), and often working the front desk — scheduling, insurance claims, and payments. Full breakdown: what does a dental assistant do and a day in the life, hour by hour.

2. Texas requirements, in plain English

The RDA, decoded.

Texas requires assistants who take X-rays to register as Registered Dental Assistants with the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners — Board-approved coursework in radiology, infection control and jurisprudence, plus an exam. No degree needed, no age limit, and about 70% of our students start with zero dental background. Details: the step-by-step registration guide and diploma/GED questions answered.
