Where the timeline came from

The "degree by 22, career by 25, house by 30" script was written for an economy that no longer exists, and even then it described averages, not requirements. Today it survives for two reasons: parents repeat the version that worked for them, and social media shows you everyone's single best day in an endless stream. Your feed is not a random sample of reality — it's a highlight reel curated by an algorithm that profits when you feel behind, because feeling behind keeps you scrolling.

What to measure instead

The only scoreboard that predicts anything

Direction and motion. Not "where am I vs. my classmates" but "am I pointed somewhere I chose, and did I move this month?" A person taking one real step monthly toward a chosen thing is doing better — measurably, in life outcomes — than a person 'on schedule' in a life they drifted into. The decision method handles the direction part; small wins handle the motion part.
