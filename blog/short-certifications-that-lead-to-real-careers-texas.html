The layer nobody showed you

Guidance counselors are measured on college placements, so the credential layer gets skipped. But Texas runs on it: dental assistants, CNAs, phlebotomists, medical assistants, pharmacy techs, HVAC techs, welders, CDL drivers, cosmetologists. Each is a licensed or certified role you can train into within weeks or months, each puts you inside an industry where you can climb, and each beats "undecided" by a mile if you'd rather earn than sit in lectures.

How to vet ANY program before paying — ours included

The three-check rule

1. License: Texas career schools must be licensed (ours is TWC #S5316 — here's how to verify any school). 2. Total cost in writing: every fee, before you sign. 3. See the room: tour before paying — real equipment and current students, or walk away. Any legitimate program passes all three without squirming.

The funding almost nobody claims

Texas Workforce Solutions can fund short-credential training for eligible people, and most eligible people never apply because nobody told them it exists. Two minutes in the free funding finder tells you if it's worth pursuing; the step-by-step voucher guide walks the process. This applies across the credential layer, not just to us.
