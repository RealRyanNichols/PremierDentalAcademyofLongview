What the tool actually is

The free practice exam has three levels. The Quick Quiz takes a few minutes and gives you a fast temperature check. The Full Practice Exam is the real workout — exam-style questions across the topics Texas tests, with an explanation after every answer so a wrong guess teaches you something instead of just stinging. The Timed Mock Exam adds the clock and the expanded question bank; it lives inside RDA Exam Pro for people who want full dress-rehearsal conditions.

One thing it is not: the official state exam. Our questions are original practice material written to teach. The official exam comes from the state — for registration requirements and official specifics, the authority is the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners.

Why practice with explanations beats rereading notes

Rereading feels productive and proves nothing. Answering a question you might get wrong — and immediately reading why — is retrieval practice, and it is the fastest honest way to find the gap between "I've seen this" and "I know this." Every question you miss on a free practice run is a question you will not be seeing for the first time on exam day.

How to use it this week

Take the Quick Quiz cold — no studying first. Whatever topic you miss most becomes your study focus for the week. Then take the Full Practice Exam on the weekend and watch the score move. Repeat. That loop is the whole secret.
