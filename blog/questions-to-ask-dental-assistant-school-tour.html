Why you should tour even if you've already decided

Websites are marketing — ours included. A tour is evidence. In one visit you learn things no page can hide: whether the equipment is real or a photo prop, whether students look engaged or bored, whether the person showing you around answers questions straight or steers you to a contract. Every legitimate school will happily let you look; hesitation to show you the room is the answer.

The money questions, specifically

Get it in writing, always

Total price including every fee. What the payment plan really totals. What happens if you withdraw. Whether the school takes Workforce Solutions funding or WIOA. A school that squirms at any of these has told you something. Our numbers live on /enroll and the tuition planner shows the exact payment math before you commit to anything.

Verify the license — it takes five minutes

Texas career schools are licensed by the Texas Workforce Commission, and the directory is public. Ours is #S5316; look it up, and look up any other school you tour. We wrote a step-by-step verification guide because this one habit filters out the programs that shouldn't be operating. While you're at it, read the red flags list — it names the sales tactics to walk away from, no matter whose lobby you're standing in.
