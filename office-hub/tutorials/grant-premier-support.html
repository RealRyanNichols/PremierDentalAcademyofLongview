Grant Premier support access

For: Owner 2 min read Last reviewed August 8, 2026

Let Premier's team look at your workspace read-only, on your terms: explicit grant, automatic expiry, full audit trail.

  1. 1.Support can't see anything by default

    Nobody at Premier can open your workspace unless you grant access. There is no standing backdoor — the grant is the only door.

  2. 2.Grants are read-only and they expire

    From Settings → Premier support, enter the support email, an optional reason, and a duration (up to 30 days, 7 by default). Support can look, never touch — and the grant shuts off by itself.

  3. 3.Every view is logged where you can see it

    Each time support opens one of your pages, a "support viewed" line is written to your audit log. You can read that log any time from Settings.

  4. 4.Revoke any time

    One click ends a grant early. Access stops immediately.
