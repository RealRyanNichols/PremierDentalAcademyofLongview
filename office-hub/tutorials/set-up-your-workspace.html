Set up your workspace

For: Owner 4 min read Last reviewed August 8, 2026

Create your practice workspace, start from a template, and get your first hire's 30/60/90 plan ready in one sitting.

  1. 1.Sign in with an email link

    Go to the hub sign-in page and enter your email. We send a one-time sign-in link — there is no password to invent or forget. Open the link on the device you'll use day to day.

  2. 2.Name your practice

    The setup wizard asks for your practice name and type (general, pediatric, ortho, oral surgery, or multi-specialty). That's all it needs — no patient systems, no integrations.

  3. 3.Start from a template

    Pick the starter plan closest to your hire's role. A template is just a pre-filled 30/60/90 plan — every task in it can be renamed, moved, or deleted, so choose the nearest fit and shape it.

  4. 4.Set the first day

    Enter the hire's start date. The plan turns day numbers into real calendar dates, so "Day 12" shows as an actual date everyone can see.

  5. 5.Make it yours

    Skim the generated plan and cut anything that isn't how your office works. A 20-task plan your team believes beats a 40-task plan nobody reads.

  6. 6.Invite your trainer first

    Before the new hire ever logs in, invite the person who will sign off skills. Trainers see the checklist and sign-off buttons; they can't change workspace settings.
