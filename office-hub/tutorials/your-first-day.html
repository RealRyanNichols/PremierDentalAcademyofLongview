Your first day in the hub

For: New hire 3 min read Last reviewed August 8, 2026

Accept your invite, find today's tasks, mark things done, and see how skill sign-offs work.

  1. 1.Accept your invite

    Open the link your office sent you and enter your email. You'll get a sign-in link by email — no password to remember. Save the hub to your phone's home screen; it works great there.

  2. 2.Your home screen is just today

    You'll see the tasks for where you are in the plan — not all ninety days at once. Read each one, do it, and mark it done. It's normal for early days to be mostly watching and reading.

  3. 3.Read the office's SOPs

    Some tasks say "read the office way" — those open your office's own steps for things like tray setups or the sterilization flow. They're short on purpose. Read them twice.

  4. 4.Quizzes are quick and open

    Knowledge checks are a few questions to confirm the important stuff stuck. Your score is recorded as pass or not — never your answer sheet.

  5. 5.Skills are signed off by your trainer

    You can't mark skill items done yourself — your trainer signs those when they've watched you do them. If a skill says "needs coaching," that's the plan for getting it right, with a date they'll check again.
