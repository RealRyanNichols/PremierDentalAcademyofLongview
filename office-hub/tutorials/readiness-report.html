Print the readiness report

For: Owner or manager 3 min read Last reviewed August 8, 2026

One page for the doctor: what's independent, what's still coached, quiz results, check-in highlights, and what's next.

  1. 1.Generate it from the hire's page

    Open the hire, choose Report, and the hub assembles the current picture: plan progress by phase, skills by state, quiz passes, and recent check-ins.

  2. 2.It's built to be printed

    Use your browser's print button — the report lays itself out for paper. Hand it to the doctor, put it in the hire's file, or bring it to a review conversation.

  3. 3.Each report is a snapshot

    Reports are saved as of the moment you generate them, so you can compare day 30 against day 60 later. Generating a new one never rewrites an old one.

  4. 4.Read the footer to the doctor

    The report footer says what this is: your practice's internal training record. It is not a certification, license, or HIPAA/OSHA credential, and it doesn't replace state-required training.
