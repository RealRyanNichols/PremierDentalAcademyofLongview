Run weekly check-ins

For: Manager 3 min read Last reviewed August 8, 2026

Capture wins, needs, and next week's plan in two minutes — and see how check-ins feed the readiness report.

  1. 1.One check-in per week, three boxes

    Wins: what clicked this week. Needs: what's wobbly. Next: the one or two things to focus on next week. Two minutes, honestly filled, beats a monthly essay.

  2. 2.Do it with the hire, not about them

    The best check-ins happen in a two-minute conversation at the end of the week — type it while you talk. The hire can read their own check-ins, so write like they're reading.

  3. 3.It feeds the readiness report

    Check-ins, task progress, and sign-offs roll into the printable readiness report — so when the doctor asks "how's the new hire doing," the answer is already written.

  4. 4.Missed a week?

    Just do this week's. A gap in the record is better than a fictional backfill.
