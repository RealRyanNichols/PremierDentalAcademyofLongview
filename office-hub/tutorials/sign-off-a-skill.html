Sign off a skill

For: Trainer 3 min read Last reviewed August 8, 2026

Move a skill from observed to independent, flag one for re-coaching with a re-check date, and understand why history can't be edited.

  1. 1.Open the hire's checklist

    From your home screen, tap the hire's name to see their skills grouped by category, with today's state on each.

  2. 2.Pick the honest state

    Observed means you watched them do it. Practicing means they're doing it with backup nearby. Independent means you'd let them do it with you out of the room. Sign off what you actually saw, not what you expect.

  3. 3.Flagging "needs coaching" is a plan, not a punishment

    When something needs to be re-done right, set the state to needs coaching, pick a re-check date, and leave one specific note ("water ratio on alginate"). The hire sees exactly what to fix and when you'll look again.

  4. 4.Every change keeps its history

    Each sign-off writes a permanent history line — who, what, when, and your note. Nobody can edit or delete history, including you. That's what makes the record trustworthy when the doctor asks.

  5. 5.What sign-offs are not

    A sign-off is your office's internal record that a skill was done to your standard. It is not a certification or license, and it never substitutes for state-required training — in Texas, x-ray positioning and exposure require the TSBDE RDA radiology certificate.
