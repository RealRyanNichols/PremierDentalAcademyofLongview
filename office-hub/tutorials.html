Onboarding hub tutorials

Everything here is a few minutes long and written in plain language. Filter by who you are in the office.

  • For: Owner 4 min

    Set up your workspace

    Create your practice workspace, start from a template, and get your first hire's 30/60/90 plan ready in one sitting.
  • For: Owner or manager 3 min

    Invite your team

    Add your manager, trainer, and new hire with role-scoped invite links, and understand what each role can see and do.
  • For: Trainer 3 min

    Sign off a skill

    Move a skill from observed to independent, flag one for re-coaching with a re-check date, and understand why history can't be edited.
  • For: New hire 3 min

    Your first day in the hub

    Accept your invite, find today's tasks, mark things done, and see how skill sign-offs work.
  • For: Manager 3 min

    Run weekly check-ins

    Capture wins, needs, and next week's plan in two minutes — and see how check-ins feed the readiness report.
  • For: Owner or manager 3 min

    Print the readiness report

    One page for the doctor: what's independent, what's still coached, quiz results, check-in highlights, and what's next.
  • For: Owner 2 min

    Grant Premier support access

    Let Premier's team look at your workspace read-only, on your terms: explicit grant, automatic expiry, full audit trail.
